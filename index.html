Honest limits — read before buying

  • Coverage bands are manufacturer figures for ideal, properly prepared surfaces — porosity, texture, thinning and application method push real consumption outside the band. Buy against the worst-case figure and treat every output as an estimate, not a guarantee.
  • Coefficients were verified on the shown date from the named documents; manufacturers reformulate and re-spec. Every value carries its verified-on date and can be overridden in one tap.
  • Quantities only, never prices — rates vary by market, dealer and day; the shop's bill is the price.
  • Suggested door/window sizes and tile-wastage percentages are stated conventions — measure your own openings; complex or diagonal lays can exceed the cited wastage.
  • This is not a site survey: dado heights, damaged plaster, extra putty coats and repair quantities are contractor judgment calls this app cannot make and does not fake.
  • Projects live only in this browser's localStorage — clearing site data erases them; print/CSV/JSON export is the only backup.
